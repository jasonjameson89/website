⚠️ Critical Interactions & Rules

Do NOT combine / separate these:

  • Rapamycin ≠ exercise same day — mTOR inhibition blunts muscle protein synthesis; separate by ≥ 24 hrs
  • Rapamycin ≠ grapefruit — Grapefruit inhibits CYP3A4 → rapamycin levels spike 4×
  • Berberine ≠ metformin (if on both) — Additive hypoglycemia risk; monitor glucose
  • Iron ≠ calcium, zinc, coffee — Compete for absorption; separate by 2 hrs
  • Vitamin D3 always with K2 — Directs calcium to bone, not arteries
  • NMN ≠ evening — Blunts nocturnal SIRT1 circadian pattern (Peek et al. 2013)
  • Fisetin cycling — Use 2 days/month (e.g. 24th–25th) at 100 mg/kg divided; daily use may be pro-survival
  • Extended fasts: electrolytes — Sodium 5–10 g/day, potassium 1–2 g/day, magnesium 400 mg — prevents hyponatremia
  • Floor sleeping / grounding — Keep bare skin contact; no socks; 15 min AM + optional 30 min PM
  • Sauna ≠ alcohol — Dehydration + vasodilation = hypotension risk

⚕️ Disclaimer

This is an aggressive protocol. Most people should start with the lifestyle pillars (OMAD, exercise, sauna, sleep, diet rotation) + a smaller supplement subset (NMN, omega-3s, D3/K2, magnesium, NAC, resveratrol) and add from there. Not medical advice. Get bloodwork and a physician's sign-off before starting rapamycin, metformin, or any Rx compound.
